# Contributing

This is a living handbook. The most useful contributions are the ones that make a number more trustworthy.

## Errata

If a number, a source, or a mechanism is wrong, open an issue with the chapter and section, what the text says, what you believe is correct, and a link to the evidence. Corrections that come with a primary source are merged quickly.

## Measurements

Several numbers in Part V were measured for this handbook on specific samples. If you rerun the scripts in `scripts/` on a different tokenizer, corpus, or engine version and get a different result, that is worth a pull request. Include:

- the tokenizer or engine version
- the corpus (name and size, or a description if private)
- the raw script output

Numbers from a single preprint are marked as such in the text. If you can reproduce or refute one, say so in an issue.

## New chapters

A chapter belongs in this volume if the failure it describes does not show up in a benchmark on a quiet GPU and does show up under real traffic, real templates, or real text. Open an issue first with the mechanism and at least one primary source before writing.

## Style

- Textbook register, English, no em dashes.
- Every chapter ends with primary sources. Aggregator and tutorial links are marked secondary.
- Charts are generated by `assets/_generate.py`. Edit the numbers there and rerun it; do not hand-edit the SVGs.
